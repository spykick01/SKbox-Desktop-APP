SKBox Remote

Play your SKBox PC on this device. You both need to be on the same Wi-Fi, and Remote play must be turned on in SKBox (Settings > Remote play).

PCs found on your Wi-Fi

Everything else (disconnect, quality, fixing a fullscreen game) is in the SKBox menu on the PC: press the Xbox or PlayStation button there, then pick Remote play. Emergency exit on this device: Ctrl+Alt+Shift+D.
